About
Liam

I'm a junior at Dartmouth's Thayer School of Engineering studying mechanical engineering with a focus on human-centered design and physical product development. I start from the human problem — understanding who is affected and why before thinking about solutions.

My work spans agricultural tool design, municipal infrastructure consulting, and urban systems research. I build things that solve real problems for real people.

Alongside engineering I study acting, anthropology, and urban geography — disciplines that shape how I observe problems and communicate across disciplines.

Dual US–Irish citizen. Based in Hanover, NH. Available in New York City.
